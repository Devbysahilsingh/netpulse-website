```text
netpulse devices [OPTIONS]
```

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `--since <SINCE>` | Period: 30m, 24h, 7d or all [default: 24h] |
| `-n, --limit <LIMIT>` | Maximum rows [default: 50] |
| `--all` | Include external (internet) hosts |

Also accepts the [global options](index.md#global-options) `--config <FILE>` and `--json`.
