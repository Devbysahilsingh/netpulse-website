!!! warning "macOS: the developer cannot be verified"
    NetPulse is not signed or notarised by Apple yet, so the first launch is blocked. Allow it once:

    - **macOS 15 Sequoia and newer:** try to open NetPulse, click **Done**, then *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway* and confirm.
    - **macOS 11–14:** in Finder → Applications, **right-click NetPulse → Open → Open**.
